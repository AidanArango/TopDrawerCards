2025-26 Topps Finest UEFA Club Competitions checklist and review

May 2026 · Set Breakdown

Collectors will notice a massive overhaul in 2025-26 Topps Finest UEFA Club Competitions Soccer. After years as a smaller release, the base checklist now spans 600 cards across common, uncommon, and rare tiers while the hobby configuration remains the same.

Base Set

600 cards split into Common 1-200, Uncommon 201-400, and Rare 401-600, with strong player coverage and base autograph depth.

Autograph Content

More than 150 signatures appear in Base Autographs, plus Prized Footballers, Arrivals, Century's Finest, Marks of Excellence, and multi-signed chase cards.

Insert Chase Cards

Collectors chase rare inserts like ’90s Madness, Debut Goals, Finest Showmen, The Man, Finest Idols, Headliners, and Aura.

2025-26 Topps Finest UEFA Checklist Highlights

A quick look at the checklist structure, parallels, and key chase content from the Beckett overview.

  • Base Set Checklist: 600 cards
  • Common: #s 1-200 • Uncommon: #s 201-400 • Rare: #s 401-600
  • 30 Common cards receive new Hologlow Variations
  • Base Autographs include 150+ players
  • Additional autograph concepts: Prized Footballers, Arrivals, Century's Finest, Marks Of Excellence
  • Multi-signed chase cards: Bloodlines and Dual Autographs
  • Inserts include Prized Footballers, ’90s Madness, Debut Goals, and Finest Showmen
  • Tough chase inserts: The Man, Finest Idols, Headliners, Aura
  • Hobby box content: 6 packs of 10 cards with 2 autographs per box
  • Release date: June 25, 2026

Base set breakdown

  • Common tier: cards #1-200
  • Uncommon tier: cards #201-400
  • Rare tier: cards #401-600
  • Early checklist examples: 1 Lamine Yamal, 2 William Saliba, 3 Frenkie de Jong, 4 Hakan Çalhanoğlu
