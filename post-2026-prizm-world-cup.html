2026 Panini Prizm World Cup Checklist and Review

June 2026 · New Set Release

The 2026 Panini Prizm World Cup set brings a strong checklist, bold Prizm parallels, and some exciting chase cards from the latest World Cup release.

Classic, colorful Base Prizm designs make this set a strong fit for new collectors and checklist builders.

Team Jerseys

Team-themed parallels and jersey inserts highlight the top squads and players across the tournament.

World Cup Stars

Key international stars are front and center, making this set especially fun for soccer fans.
