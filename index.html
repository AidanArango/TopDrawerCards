About This Collection

Welcome to my personal sports card collection showcase. I've been collecting graded and raw cards for years, with a focus on men's and women's soccer, baseball, other sports, and some TCG.

This collection represents my passion for the hobby and appreciation for the artistry of sports cards and not just the dollar amount.
